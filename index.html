Babette's Feat

“Here it's never about costs. Only about the law.”

You are Babette, research manager. You have a PhD and an office between the professors and the administration.

Your job is to get research done. Twelve requests will cross your desk this year. Each has two to four ways through, and each way does three things:

Research. How much of what the project needs survives. This is what you are for. The year is worth up to 1,000 points, and 850 would be a feat.
Budget. Your unit has for the year. Doing things properly is often the dear way. What is left at the end is yours to place.
Risk. What an auditor would make of the file, if he ever opened it.

At the end of the year the auditors come for one afternoon. They pull two of your twelve files, and each gets a spin of its own wheel. The red on the wheel is the risk you left in the file. If a wheel stops on red, you are not sacked. You are moved to the library.

Beware: some ways through only sound clever.
